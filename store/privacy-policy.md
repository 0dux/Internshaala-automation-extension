# Privacy Policy — Internshala Saved Filters

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Internshala Saved Filters is a browser extension that saves Internshala search filters on your own device. This policy explains what is stored and what is not.

## What the extension stores

The extension stores the following in your browser's `localStorage` for `internshala.com`:

- The URL and name of your last filtered internship or job search.
- Saved preset names, URLs, and timestamps.
- A single on/off setting for automatically opening the last search.

This information stays in your browser. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

## What the extension does not do

- No account or login.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising, or profiling.
- No network requests.
- No access to your Internshala account, applications, messages, resumes, or credentials.
- No selling, renting, or sharing of data.

## Export and import

The Export button creates a JSON file on your device. You choose where to save it. The Import button reads only the file you select. Neither action uploads anything.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual presets in the panel, clear the last search with the Clear button, clear Internshala site data in your browser settings, or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling the extension may not remove data already stored in the site's `localStorage`; clearing site data will.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the extension listing and the date above will change.
